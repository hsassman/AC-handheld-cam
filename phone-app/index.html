Fullscreen (hide address bar)
Fullscreen hides the browser's address bar, and with it your PC's IP. On iPhone, Safari can't go fullscreen from a page: use Share → Add to Home Screen once and open it from the home screen instead, which drops the address bar entirely.

Movement (AR · 6DoF)

Android Chrome only. Uses your phone's AR tracking so physically stepping or leaning moves the in-game camera: closer to objects, or out a window. It also removes the near-upright flip. iPhone isn't supported (Safari has no WebXR AR); the gyro still works there.
